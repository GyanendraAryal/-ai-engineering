# EMPLOYEE BENEFITS & WORKPLACE POLICY SUMMARY

## 📊 1. Annual Leave & Time Off
* **Paid Leave Entitlement:** All employees are entitled to **24 days of paid leave per calendar year**.
* **Tracking & Approval:** Leave requests should be planned in advance and submitted through the company portal for management approval.

## 🏢 2. Work Schedule & Attendance Model
* **Hybrid Work Framework:** The company operates on a structured hybrid model.
* **In-Office Days:** Attendance in the physical office is mandatory every **Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday**.
* **Remote Work Flexibility:** Employees have the option to work from home on Mondays and Fridays, provided performance standards are met.

## 💰 3. Monthly Allowances & Reimbursements
Employees can utilize the following monthly stipends to support their health, well-being, and remote working infrastructure:
* **Gym & Fitness Reimbursement:** Up to **4,000 per month** can be claimed for gym memberships, fitness classes, or health club fees.
* **Home Internet Allowance:** A stipend of up to **2,000 per month** is available to cover high-speed home internet costs incurred for remote work.
* *Note: All claims must be accompanied by valid invoices/receipts submitted before the monthly payroll cutoff.*

## 📝 4. Separation & Notice Period
* **Notice Requirement:** A standard **90-day notice period** applies to both the employee and the company to ensure a smooth transition of responsibilities during separation.
